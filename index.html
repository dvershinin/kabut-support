Kabut ☀️

Real AQI. Ridiculous reactions.

A free, noncommercial iPhone, Apple Watch and Android phone app with regional air-quality estimates for Indonesian cities, expressive sun characters and Indonesian jokes.

Support

Questions or a bug? Email ciapnz@gmail.com. Include your device model, app version and what happened. Screenshots are optional; remove personal information before sending them.

Choose a city in the phone app, check the model time, and tap refresh for another request. Android currently has no Wear OS companion. Sharing happens only through the system share sheet after you choose it.

On iPhone, your paired Apple Watch receives the selected city and can also fetch independently. Add “Kabut: keluar nggak?” in your Watch face's complication picker. Availability depends on the face and complication slot. Tap the complication for details. Background refresh is scheduled by watchOS and is not continuous.

AQI and PM2.5 are regional model estimates from Open-Meteo and CAMS, not neighbourhood sensor readings. Check the displayed model time and stale warning. The sun, sky resolutions and outdoor verdicts are satire based on AQI only, not weather forecasts or medical advice. Follow local official guidance for personal safety.

Privacy policy

Effective 20 October 2026. Kabut is provided by GetPageSpeed LLC. Published on Google Play by Smartycode Labs, a GetPageSpeed LLC developer account.

Kabut has no account, advertising, tracking or analytics SDK. It does not request your GPS location, contacts, photos, microphone or health data. City choice, recent jokes and cached air-quality readings remain in app-private storage. Android backup and device transfer are disabled. The iPhone sends the selected city, reading and joke to its paired Watch through Apple's WatchConnectivity, and the Watch complication uses a local App Group cache.

To retrieve air quality, the phone, Watch or complication connects directly to Open-Meteo over HTTPS. Requests include the fixed coordinates of the chosen city and your network IP address. They do not include an advertising ID, hardware identifier, account identifier or your GPS location. Open-Meteo states that it may retain technical server logs, including IP addresses and request coordinates, for up to 90 days for maintenance and abuse prevention, without linking them to user identities. Read Open-Meteo's privacy terms. These technical request data are necessary for app functionality and are not used by Kabut for tracking.

Sharing happens only when you choose the share button and a destination in the system share sheet. The receiving app or person then handles that card under their own practices. If you email support, we receive the content and email address you choose to send and use them to answer your request.

To remove Kabut's local app data, delete the app from your phone and Watch. There is no account to delete. For privacy questions or requests about support correspondence, email ciapnz@gmail.com. Open-Meteo handles its own service logs under its policy. Apple and Google handle their respective store transactions, optional platform diagnostics and device services under their own policies.

Changes to this policy will appear on this page with an updated effective date.
